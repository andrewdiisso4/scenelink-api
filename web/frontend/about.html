About SceneLink

Connecting people through exceptional dining and social experiences

Our Story

SceneLink was born from a simple idea: make discovering great food and planning social outings effortless and fun. We noticed that friends spent countless hours texting back and forth, trying to decide where to eat, when to meet, and what everyone wanted to do.

Founded in Boston in 2025, SceneLink is an early-stage local startup building a social-first discovery platform for the city’s restaurants, bars, and nightlife. We’re here to help you and your friends create memorable experiences together.

Our Mission

We're on a mission to transform how people discover and experience hospitality. By combining intelligent recommendations with social connections, we make it easy to find amazing places, coordinate with friends, and create lasting memories.

What Makes Us Different

Social First

Everything we build is designed to help you connect with friends and share experiences.

AI-Powered Discovery

Our smart recommendations learn your preferences and help you discover places you'll love.

Local Focus

We're passionate about supporting local businesses and helping you discover the best in your neighborhood.
